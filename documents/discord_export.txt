Message: finishing up junior year pre-med here. quick professor breakdown for anyone who needs it. Barry in SPH is the best public health professor on campus, fills up instantly so set an alarm for registration. Clendenin for EPIB 301 is genuinely hard but she prepares you for actual public health work and her feedback is the best you will get at TAMU. DeLeon is rough — if you have a choice between her and Barry for the same HLTH course always take Barry. Goodey for orgo is non-negotiable, do not take anyone else if you care about your GPA. Herman for BIMS 301 if you want MCAT prep, Ory if you need GPA protection.
