Professor: Catherine Cubbin
Department: Public Health
Course: HLTH 370
Rating: 4.2
Date: 2024-12-20
Review: Cubbin for social determinants of health is one of the most thought-provoking courses I took at TAMU. She brings in cutting-edge research on health inequities and challenges you to think about structural factors in health. The reading load is heavy — expect 50 to 80 pages per week — but the discussion-based class format makes it worth it. Her exams are essay-based and reward original thinking over regurgitation. Strong recommend for public health majors who want depth.
