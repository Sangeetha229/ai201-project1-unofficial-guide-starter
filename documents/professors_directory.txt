Professor: Angela Clendenin
Department: Public Health
Course: EPIB 301
Source: Professors.directory
Date: 2024-12-20
Review: Dr Clendenin's Introduction to Epidemiology is one of the most demanding and most rewarding courses available to undergraduates at TAMU. Her standards are high but her expectations are fully transparent from the first day of class. She provides detailed written feedback on all major assignments, and her grading rubric is posted publicly on the syllabus. Students who engage seriously with the reading and attend lecture consistently perform well. She is particularly strong at helping students understand causal inference and study design. Excellent preparation for graduate public health programs and medical school interviews.

Professor: Brian Colwell
Department: Public Health
Course: HLTH 310
Source: Professors.directory
Date: 2024-09-18
Review: Colwell is an outstanding health communication professor who brings both academic rigor and real-world relevance to every lecture. He has significant experience with public health campaigns and brings that perspective into the classroom directly. His exams are straightforward for students who attend lecture and complete the readings. His feedback on research papers is constructive and detailed. Office hours are productive and he treats students as capable of complex thinking. Highly recommended for any pre-med student wanting to understand patient communication theory.

Professor: Jim Herman
Department: BIMS
Course: BIMS 301
Source: Professors.directory
Date: 2025-01-28
Review: Herman's BIMS 301 is widely regarded as one of the most challenging undergraduate courses in the TAMU pre-med pathway and also one of the most valuable. His application-focused exam style forces students to develop genuine understanding rather than surface memorization. Students who complete his course consistently report improved performance on MCAT biological sciences content. He curves assessments when class performance warrants it and his office hours are genuinely informative. Not the right choice for students looking for an easy GPA boost, but excellent for students committed to medical school preparation.

Professor: Joanna Goodey
Department: Chemistry
Course: CHEM 227
Source: Professors.directory
Date: 2024-11-30
Review: Goodey is widely considered the best professor for Organic Chemistry 1 at Texas A&M. Her systematic approach to teaching reaction mechanisms builds student understanding incrementally. Her practice exams closely mirror real exam content and are available two weeks before each test. She provides meaningful curves at semester end based on class performance. Her office hours are well-attended and she and her TAs maintain consistent availability throughout the semester. Her pass rate and student satisfaction ratings are among the highest for any chemistry section at TAMU.
